Common questions

FAQ

We already have ChatGPT — why do we need this?

ChatGPT is a tool. What's missing is consistency — the same process, the same output, every time, regardless of who's running it. We build workflows and agents purpose-built for your operations, validated before deployment, and documented so your whole team uses them the same way.

What happens to our client data?

We work within your existing systems. Client data doesn't move outside your environment. Every engagement includes a PDPA-compliant data processing agreement before any work begins.

How long before we see results?

We measure from day one — hours saved, output quality, before and after. You won't be waiting for a quarterly review to know if it's working.

What if our team doesn't actually use it?

Adoption is part of the build, not an afterthought. We work through the workflow with your team during implementation. Staff onboarding and documentation are included in every engagement — so they run it independently when we leave.
